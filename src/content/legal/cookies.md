This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Statistics

We may measure how the website is used with [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io/data-policy), which is hosted in the EU. It sets no cookies and does not identify you: it produces aggregate statistics only, such as how many people visited a page, which site they came from and whether they went on to apply for early access. It stores nothing on your device unless you turn statistics off. If you block its script, the website works exactly the same.

### Turn statistics off

You can stop your visits being counted in this browser. Your choice applies from the next page you open, and lasts until you turn statistics back on or clear this website’s data from your browser.

## Strictly necessary storage

If a page needs to remember something to work, it is stored only for that purpose.

If you turn statistics off, your browser keeps one note in its local storage, named “plausible_ignore”, which tells Plausible to ignore your visits. It holds nothing about you, and it is the only thing this website stores for statistics.

## Applying for early access

The “Apply for early access” button takes you to the CloudBusiness account site at [account.cloudbusiness.software](https://account.cloudbusiness.software), where you fill in your application. That site has its own cookie and privacy information.

## The help centre

Our help centre at [help.firstmilemanager.co.uk](https://help.firstmilemanager.co.uk) sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and doesn’t use Plausible. Our [privacy notice](/privacy#the-help-centre) says what it records and for how long.
