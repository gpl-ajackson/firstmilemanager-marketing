## The help centre

Our help centre at [help.firstmilemanager.co.uk](https://help.firstmilemanager.co.uk) sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and does not use Plausible or any other statistics script. To improve our help it keeps four kinds of record. None of them records who you are: no IP address, device, account or company, and nothing that tells one reader from another. The only words kept are search words, cleaned as described below, and any feedback comment you choose to write.

| Record | What it holds | How long we keep it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search Records | The words of each search, with anything that looks like an email address, phone number, UK postcode or long reference number replaced by a marker such as “[email]” before it is saved; how many results were shown; whether you opened one, and which; the date, with no time; and where the search was made | 13 months |
| Article Feedback | Your answer to “Did this answer your question?”; if you answered no, the reason you chose and any comment you wrote; the article; the date the article was last checked; the date, with no time; and where the feedback was given | Answers and reasons 13 months; comments 6 months, or sooner if we delete one |
| Page View Counts | How many times each page was viewed each day: a number for each page and day, and nothing else | Kept for good, as they hold nothing about anyone |
| Still Stuck Counts | How many times each article’s “Still stuck?” link was used each day: a number for each article and day, and nothing else | Kept for good, as they hold nothing about anyone |

Each month we add the records up into totals, which hold no search words or comments, and keep those totals for good so we can compare a month with the same month a year earlier.

Please don’t put names, email addresses or order details in a feedback comment. If you think you have, email [hello@firstmilemanager.co.uk](mailto:hello@firstmilemanager.co.uk) with the article and the date and we will delete the comment. When you answer that an article doesn’t match what you see, your comment is also copied into a private issue in our GitHub account, so the person who looks after that article can put it right the same day. We delete that issue, with every comment copied into it, six months after it was opened. “Still stuck?” opens your own email program with a message to us; once you send it, the email is handled as described above.

The help centre runs on its own server in London, a Laravel VPS provided by Laravel Holdings Inc. on DigitalOcean’s infrastructure. Its search runs on the same server, so the words you search for are not sent to any other company. Its traffic passes through Cloudflare, which protects it from attacks. As with any website, Cloudflare and Laravel see visitors’ IP addresses in their own logs; we don’t copy them into our records. Each night the help centre’s records are backed up to Cloudflare R2 storage in the EU, and each backup is deleted after 14 days. Laravel, DigitalOcean and Cloudflare may process data outside the UK, under safeguards approved for international transfers. GitHub, Inc. stores copied feedback comments in the United States, under safeguards approved for international transfers.
