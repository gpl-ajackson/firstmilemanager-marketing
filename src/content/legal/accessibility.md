We want everyone to be able to use this website, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we do

- Every page works with a keyboard, and has a visible focus outline and a link to skip to the main content.
- Text and controls are designed to meet WCAG AA colour contrast.
- Pages are built with headings, landmarks, labelled links and table headers so screen readers can navigate them.
- Pages work at narrow widths and when text is enlarged, without scrolling sideways.
- Animations respect your device’s reduced motion setting.

## Tell us about a problem

If something on this website is hard to use, email [hello@firstmilemanager.co.uk](mailto:hello@firstmilemanager.co.uk) with the page and what happened, and we’ll fix it or find another way to get you the information.
